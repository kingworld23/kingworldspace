Best Practices for Effective Fertilizer Application (उर्वरक उपयोग की विधि)

01

Soil Health Card

Always test soil pH and N-P-K reserves before buying fertilizers to avoid excess application.

02

Basal Dose Timing

Apply entire dose of Phosphorus (DAP/SSP) and Potash at the time of field preparation/sowing.

03

Split Urea Application

Divide Urea into 2 or 3 splits: at tillering stage and panicle initiation to prevent leaching loss.

04

Immediate Irrigation

Irrigate lightly right after top dressing so chemical nutrients dissolve directly to root zone.

05

Organic Co-Use

Mix chemical fertilizers with organic farmyard manure (FYM) to maintain earthworm activity.
